# Attendance branding

Website name: **Mountain Provinces Mission Office Attendance**.
Android app name: **MPM Attendance**, including its login screen, scanner title, and Android launcher label.

The logo is the official Seventh-day Adventist symbol from the [Adventist Identity Guideline System](https://www.adventist.design/downloads/logo/). The original white and forest SVGs are bundled in `web/images/`, and the original forest PNG is bundled in `scanner/assets/sda-logo.png`. All assets are served or loaded locally for use on the office network.

Source archives:

- [Official symbol SVGs](https://cdn.adventist.org/adventist-logo/2/symbol/adventist-symbol-svg--symbol.zip)
- [Official symbol PNGs](https://cdn.adventist.org/adventist-logo/2/symbol/adventist-symbol-png--symbol.zip)

Android launcher icons are resized from the supplied PNG, preserving the artwork and its clear space on a white background. Adaptive icons include additional padding so Android launcher masks do not crop the symbol.

The dashboard's website name is fixed branding. The editable office name in Settings remains the office name used on employee IDs and reports; new databases default to Mountain Provinces Mission Office Attendance.
